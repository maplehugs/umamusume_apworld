# Showcase

A seed in progress, played on a local room.

## In the game

**Only unlocked scenarios can be picked.** This seed has URA Finale (starting
scenario) and Grand Live (received as an item); Unity Cup and Trackblazer stay
hidden until they arrive.

![Scenario select showing only the unlocked scenarios](media/scenario-select.gif)

![Grand Live, received as an item, next to URA Finale](media/game-scenario-grand-live.png)

([mp4](media/scenario-select.mp4))

**Each seed starts you with its own random trainees.** Only those, and the
ones you receive as items (here Kitasan Black), can start a career; everyone
else stays unscouted.

![Trainee select: the seed's starters plus a received trainee](media/game-trainee-select.png)

**Items arrive as presents.** Trainees and support cards land in the gift box,
marked with who sent them.

![Presents from Archipelago](media/game-presents.png)

| Trainee, with her outfit | SSR support card |
|---|---|
| ![Trainee collected](media/game-trainee-outfit.png) | ![SSR collected](media/game-ssr-collected.png) |

**Rainbow Uncap Crystals limit-break the SSR of your choice** (Enhance > Support
Cards > Limit Break), one level each, up to four. Here Kitasan Black after two.

![Kitasan Black uncapped twice with crystals](media/game-uncap-crystal.png)

**A Trainee Awakening takes a random trainee you own to 5 stars and potential 5.** Before and after, on the trainee select:

| Before | After |
|---|---|
| ![Starters at 3 stars, potential 1](media/game-awaken-before.png) | ![Satono Diamond at 5 stars, potential 5](media/game-awaken-after.png) |


## The web panel

The server's panel (<http://localhost:8189>) is where you connect to the room.
It shows the seed, the goal, your buffs, traps and boons, and lets you ask for hints.

![Connection, room and permanent buffs](media/panel-connection.png)

**Items**: the checks you sent and the items you received, with who they came from.

![Sent and received items, hints](media/panel-items.png)

**Tracker**: every check, colored by what logic says you can do now.

![Check tracker](media/panel-tracker.png)
